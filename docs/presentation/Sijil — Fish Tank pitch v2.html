How it works

One proxy. Four things, on every call.

Your applicationUNCHANGED
Sijil proxyOPENAI-COMPATIBLE · <1 MS
Your model endpointUNCHANGED · YOUR HARDWARE
01

Check

Evaluated against the policy in force. Record-only or enforce — the entity chooses, per rule.

02

Seal

Model digest, node & region, policy hash, approval ref, input/output hashes. Ed25519-signed, chained to the record before it.

03

Verify

An independent checker — separate code, no shared imports — walks the chain and passes, or names the exact record that broke.

04

Report

A signed A4 document for the period: calls by decision, policy versions, verifier result, every flag and block.
