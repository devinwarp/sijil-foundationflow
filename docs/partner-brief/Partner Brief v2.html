What it is

Sijil seals a tamper-evident record of every LLM inference — model version, execution node and location, policy in force, approval reference, input and output hashes — on infrastructure the customer owns. An independent verifier proves the chain is unbroken. The output is an audit report an assurance function can hand to a third party.

The accounts this is for

NOT THE ONES YOU TAKE WHOLE

Platform adopters

Where a customer adopts your platform end to end, they get lineage and audit with it. Sijil is redundant there, and we will say so. We are channel, not competition.

THE ONES YOU CAN'T

Stack owners and mixed estates

Customers who built their own inference stack, or run an estate they will not replace, have no attestation path — hyperscaler confidential inference does not reach hardware they own. Nobody replaces a working stack to get an audit trail, so the requirement goes unmet and the bid gets harder. Sijil covers these accounts without displacing anything you would otherwise sell.

Honest boundaries

Sijil proves records have not been altered since sealing. It does not yet prove the sealer itself was honest, and node location is signed rather than hardware-bound. Hardware root of trust — binding the seal to the silicon — is roadmap, built on NVIDIA and TEE attestation primitives rather than replacing them. Priced per deployed model, per year. We say all of this to customers too.
